1. summarize_with_aspects.txt 

You are given a patient story from a doctor’s clinical note. 
Read the text carefully and produce a structured summary in five parts. 
Write each part as a separate short paragraph. 

IMPORTANT: Base your summary strictly on the provided content. Do not add or infer any information. 
If the text does not mention a particular aspect, leave that section empty. 

Aspects to cover: 
1. Patient history: Summarise the relevant background and history of the patient. 
2. Doctor’s examination: Summarise what the doctor examined or tested. 
3. Doctor’s findings: Summarise what the doctor found or observed. 
4. Doctor’s diagnosis: Summarise the doctor’s diagnostic conclusion. 
5. Treatment plan: Summarise the treatment or management plan proposed. 

Example:
---
Patient report:
Cause of outcome: Cough.
Background: 32-year-old generally healthy woman. General respiratory symptoms for 5-6 days, now cough is emphasized, mild flank pain with coughing fits. No fever, ear or sinus symptoms.
Current status: Good. Breathing free, calm. Heart rate steady, no rhythm. RR 127/80 p 70. Mild mucus rales in the lungs. Ttymp 36.5. Spo2 98%. Rapid CRP 16.
Plan: Impression of viral bronchitis, symptomatic treatment. Speech work: sva 3 days, new connection required.
Diagnosis: J20.9 Unspecified acute bronchitis.

Summary:
1. The patient has had upper respiratory symptoms such as cough and mild flank pain for a few days. He does not have a fever or symptoms of the ears or sinuses.
2. The doctor performed a clinical examination, which included auscultation of the heart and lungs. The doctor also measured blood pressure, fever, oxygen saturation, and inflammation level.
3. Mild rales were heard in the lungs. The inflammation level was slightly elevated. No other abnormal findings were observed on the examination.
4. The doctor diagnosed viral bronchitis.
5. The doctor recommended symptomatic treatment and wrote a sick leave certificate.
---
Now summarize the following text:
{{NOTE_TEXT}}

Print only five parts in the order given, each as a separate copy.
